Three steps between an annoyance and a fix

Wandful compiles your circle plus your words into a small CSS spell scoped to the site you're on. You can inspect, pause or delete every spell — nothing happens that you didn't draw.

01 — CAST

Press the wand

Click the Wandful icon (or Alt+Shift+W) on any site. The page dims; your cursor becomes a lasso.

02 — CIRCLE

Circle what you mean

Draw around one element or slash through a whole row. Overlap the lasso to narrow: 40 targets → 12 → exactly the ones you want. Wandful offers “…and 28 more like them?” when a pattern is clear.

03 — SAY IT

Describe the change

“Remove all of these.” “Make this bigger.” “Blur this until I click.” Cast — it applies instantly and sticks on every reload. Manage everything in your Spellbook.

Questions

Is Wandful free?

Yes. Unlimited spells, the recipe library and circle-and-describe casting with built-in recipes are free forever. Pro ($4/mo) adds hosted AI inference so you don't need your own API key.

How is this different from Tampermonkey or Stylus?

Those are excellent tools for people who write code. Wandful is for people who don't: you circle what you mean instead of writing selectors, and spells are stored with their intent and a fingerprint — when a site redesigns, a spell can be re-cast with one click instead of silently dying.

What does Wandful have access to?

Only sites you've cast on, and only after you grant that site. We never request “all sites” at install. Your API key (if you add one) stays in your browser's local storage and is sent only to your chosen provider. We run no proxy in the free tier.

Can a spell run JavaScript on pages?

Only if you opt in: the Advanced tier uses Chrome's user-scripts switch, clearly labeled per spell. By default spells are pure CSS — inert by construction.

Where do my spells live?

On your machine, in Chrome's local storage. Export them as JSON any time from the Spellbook or settings.

Install Wandful

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi (any Chromium browser):

  1. Download wandful-extension.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Open any website, press the 🪄 icon — or Alt+Shift+W — and cast your first spell.
