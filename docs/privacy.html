🪄 Wandful — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

The short version

Wandful collects nothing. Your spells, your API key, and your settings live in your browser's local storage on your machine. We operate no accounts, no analytics, and no server that sees your browsing.

What Wandful stores, and where

  • Spells (your saved edits: selectors, a text fingerprint of the target element, your intent text, optional trimmed HTML samples, and the generated CSS) are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device. A slim index (site, intent, on/off state) syncs via your browser's built-in chrome.storage.sync if you are signed into Chrome — that is Google's infrastructure, not ours.
  • Your AI API key, if you add one, is stored locally and sent only to the provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, or OpenRouter) when you cast a spell. Wandful's publisher never sees it and never proxies it.

What leaves your machine

  • If you use bring-your-own-key: the circled elements' trimmed HTML samples, a computed-style digest, and your intent text are sent directly from your browser to your chosen AI provider to generate CSS. That provider's privacy policy then applies.
  • If you use the built-in recipes and heuristics: nothing leaves your machine at all.
  • Wandful's publisher collects no telemetry, no crash reports and no usage statistics.

Permissions

Wandful asks for activeTab (so the wand can read the page when you invoke it), scripting and storage. Host permissions are requested per site, only when you first cast a spell on that site — never for all sites at install. Optional advanced JavaScript spells use Chrome's user-scripts switch, which you control.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, transfer, or use your data for any purpose unrelated to Wandful's single purpose, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness. There is no data to sell: it never reaches us.
